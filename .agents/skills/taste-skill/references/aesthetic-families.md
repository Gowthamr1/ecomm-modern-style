# Aesthetic Families Reference

Each aesthetic family provides a complete design direction with typography, colors, layout patterns, and animation rules.

## 1. Editorial Monolith (Luxury & Curated E-Commerce)
- **Typography**: 
  - Headlines: High-contrast editorial serif (`Playfair Display`, `Fraunces`, `Cinzel`, `Bodoni Moda`).
  - Subheadings & Body: Modern neutral sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, `General Sans`).
  - Small labels: Uppercase tracked sans (`letter-spacing: 0.1em`).
- **Color**:
  - Background: Cream/Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) or Deep Onyx (`#0A0A0A`).
  - Text: Charcoal Black (`#111111`) or Soft Alabaster (`#F5F5F5`).
  - Accent: Rich Bordeaux (`#722F37`), Forest Olive (`#2D382E`), or Burnt Ochre (`#C86D51`).
- **Layout**: Asymmetric grids, offset images, large hero typographic banners with subtle opacity layering.

## 2. Industrial Cyber (Developer & Infrastructure)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings & Data: Monospaced display (`JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `Space Mono`).
  - Body: Precision geometric sans (`Geist Sans`, `Inter`).
- **Color**:
  - Background: Deep Zinc (`#09090B`) or Slate Black (`#0B0F17`).
  - Borders: Hairline translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
  - Accents: Terminal Emerald (`#10B981`), Amber Telemetry (`#F59E0B`), Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`).
- **Layout**: Dense data tables, status pill indicators, real-time counters, terminal windows.

## 3. Crafted Tactile (Humanist & Studio)
- **Typography**: `Newsreader` or `Spectral` paired with `Instrument Sans`.
- **Color**: Warm Paper (`#F7F5F0`), Deep Taupe (`#2E2C29`), Soft Clay (`#D4A373`).
- **Layout**: Soft pill tags, gentle 1px card borders with subtle inner shadows (`shadow-[inset_0_1px_0_0_rgba(255,255,255,0.6)]`), rounded corners (`rounded-xl`).
